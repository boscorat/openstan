# Feedback

openstan is developed in the open and shaped by real user feedback. Whether
you hit a rough edge during installation, found a bank that doesn't parse
correctly, or have an idea for a feature that would save you hours — we want
to hear about it.

## What to Share

Your feedback is most useful when it is specific. Some prompts:

- **Installation** — did the installer work on your platform, or did you
  hit any problems getting started?
- **Import quality** — did your bank statements parse correctly, or were
  there errors, missing fields, or REVIEW/FAILURE results?
- **Export & reports** — did the Excel or CSV output meet your needs?
- **Feature requests** — is there something missing that would save you
  time? Describe the workflow you are trying to complete.
- **General experience** — anything that felt confusing, slow, or broken.

## Bug Reports and Bank Requests

For specific bugs or to request support for a new bank, the following
channels give your report more visibility and allow others to track progress:

- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/boscorat/openstan/issues)** — report
  bugs with detailed steps to reproduce
- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/boscorat/openstan/discussions)** —
  request a new bank or discuss a feature idea with the community

If you are reporting a parsing failure, the [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md)
explains how to capture logs and produce an anonymised statement safe to
attach to an issue. The [Logging & Debugging Guide](guides/logging.md) shows how to
enable verbose mode for detailed diagnostics before filing a report.
