# Community & Support

openstan is built by a small team and maintained in the open. Here is how to
get help, stay updated, and connect with us.

---

## Get help

| Channel | Best for |
|---------|----------|
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/boscorat/openstan/discussions) | Questions, troubleshooting, "how do I …?" |
| [Documentation](index.md) | Guides, tutorials, screen references |
| [Video Tutorials](video-tutorials.md) | Step-by-step walkthroughs |

**GitHub Discussions** is our primary support channel. Search existing
discussions before posting — your question may already be answered. We aim to
respond within 7 days.

---

## Request a new bank

Don't see your bank? You can [request it on GitHub](https://github.com/boscorat/bank_statement_parser/issues/new?template=new-bank-request.yml)
or vote on existing requests in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/boscorat/openstan/discussions) under
the **Adding a New Bank** category — request a new bank, discuss bank support,
or ask how to add a bank configuration yourself. Vote on existing requests to
help us prioritise.

New banks are added via [TOML configuration files](https://boscorat.github.io/bank_statement_parser/latest/guides/new-bank-config/)
— no coding required.

## Response expectations

openstan is maintained alongside full-time work. We do not promise real-time
responses. Our target is:

- **GitHub Discussions:** within 7 days
- **GitHub Issues:** within 14 days
- **Social media:** best effort

If something is urgent, tag it as such in your discussion title and we will do
our best to prioritise.
