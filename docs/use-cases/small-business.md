# Small Business Bookkeeping

If you run a small business, your bank statements are the foundation
of your bookkeeping. openstan imports your business bank statement
PDFs, creates a searchable transaction database, and exports clean
data for your accounting software — all without sending your
financial data to the cloud.

[Quick Start Guide](../quickstart.md){ .md-button }

## The Challenge

### PDF Statements Aren't Bookkeeping

Banks give you PDFs. Your accountant or bookkeeper needs structured
data — dates, descriptions, amounts, categories. Bridging that gap
manually takes hours every month.

### Multiple Business Accounts

Many businesses run separate accounts for different purposes —
operating, tax savings, payroll. Each needs tracking.

### Making Tax Digital

From April 2026, sole traders earning over £50,000 must keep
digital records and submit quarterly updates to HMRC. Your records
need to be in a structured, digital format — not paper PDFs.

---

## How openstan Helps

### Import Business Statements

Drop your business bank statement PDFs into openstan. It parses
every transaction and stores it in a local database.

### Build a Transaction Database

Your transactions become a searchable, filterable database — not
just a spreadsheet. Search by date range, amount, description, or
account.

### Export for Your Accountant

Export to Excel, CSV, or JSON. Use the flat table for simple needs,
or the full star-schema dataset (accounts, dates, transactions,
balances) for comprehensive bookkeeping.

### Use Reports for Monthly Reviews

Build saved report configurations for your monthly bookkeeping
routine — filter by date range, group by account, and calculate
running totals.

## Typical Workflow

1. **[Install openstan](../installation.md)** on your computer
2. **Create a project** for your business
3. **Download statement PDFs** from your business bank account(s)
4. **Import monthly** — it takes under a minute
5. **Export to CSV** and pass to your bookkeeper, or import into
   your accounting software
6. **Build reports** for monthly management reviews
