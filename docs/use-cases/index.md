# Use Cases

openstan helps different people do different things with their bank
statement PDFs. Find your use case below.

## For Individuals

[**Self Assessment**](self-assessment.md)
Import 12 months of statements, separate business from personal
transactions, and export clean data for your tax return.

[**Multi-Account Tracking**](multi-account-tracking.md)
Combine statements from multiple bank accounts and credit cards into
a single searchable dataset — no cloud, no Open Banking.

## For Professionals

[**Probate & Estate Administration**](probate.md)
Convert PDF statements from the deceased's accounts into structured
spreadsheets for estate accounts, IHT406, and probate administration.

[**Conveyancing & Source of Funds**](conveyancing.md)
Verify deposits, trace funds, and check statement continuity for
source of funds enquiries.

[**Small Business**](small-business.md)
Import business account statements, build a transaction database,
and export for your bookkeeper or accounting software.
